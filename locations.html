Southern California Retail Logistics FAQ

Q: Why is a Commerce and Santa Fe Springs footprint ideal for retail apparel compliance?
A: Operating out of Commerce (90040) and Santa Fe Springs (90670) places inventory directly adjacent to the major Los Angeles rail yards and routing corridors. This minimizes drayage transit from the Ports of LA and Long Beach, allowing apparel brands to meet strict compliance windows for cross-docking and big-box distribution.

Q: Can you handle EDI routing and strict compliance guides for Target and Walmart?
A: Yes. Our specialized retail apparel infrastructure handles full Electronic Data Interchange (EDI) order processing, specialized retail ticketing, and precise flat-pack sorting to ensure zero chargebacks from major retailers like Walmart, Target, Costco, and Sam's Club.
